For truckload carriers

Valora by ANIVA AI

A network value creation application for truckload operations — pricing, network balance, load selection and customer profitability in one place.

Load value TSC Total System Contribution
=
Profit on the load revenue − cost
+
Network value at destination where the truck ends up
−
Network value at origin where the truck started

Measured per truck-day and per load, for every market — not rate per mile.

The idea

Rate per mile hides where a fleet actually makes money. Valora measures every load by its Total System Contribution — the profit on the load plus the change in network value between where it ends and where it starts — per truck-day and per load, for every market.

What it delivers

  • Network value per truck-day for every market and lane
  • A weekly-refreshed reprice list and customer ranking by net opportunity
  • Market imbalance analysis with surplus / shortfall pairing for sales and dispatch
  • Round-trip lane-pair scoring and shipper dwell benchmarking
  • A live dispatch menu that rescores as a rate is typed

For shippers running truckload bids

Optara by ANIVA AI

Truckload carrier bidding, selection and execution — end to end. Send the bid, rank rates and capacity, negotiate rounds you can measure, award within commitments, and hand your TMS the routing guide.

1 Plan→ 2 Build files→ 3 Invite→ 4 Round 1→ 5 Targets→ 6 Round 2→ 7 Award→ 8 Execute

One tool, eight steps — every step a screen, every event one auditable database.

The idea

The annual truckload bid outgrew its tools: hundreds of lanes, dozens of carriers, thousands of quotes — run through email threads and one master spreadsheet, and awarded on rate alone. Optara awards rates and capacity: the waterfall builds the routing guide itself — Primary, Secondary, Tertiary, every carrier at their own quoted rate, never past their own commitment.

What it delivers

  • A carrier-safe, seven-sheet bid package sent in one click
  • Every response screened and validated before it touches the rankings
  • Negotiation rounds with lane-by-lane targets — movement measured, not guessed
  • An award with a full audit trail and savings you can defend
  • A TMS-ready routing guide, EDI onboarding order and a carrier compliance gate

For specialized field services

Labora by ANIVA AI

Labor demand planning and workforce optimization for companies engaged in specialized field services — fixed and variable staff to meet seasonality and the forecast workload, not the average week.

The idea

Field service demand is seasonal and lumpy — peak months overwhelm the crews while the off-season carries their cost. Labora plans the workforce from the demand itself: forecast the workload by territory and season, translate it into hours by skill, and set the fixed core and the variable flex around the real curve.

What it delivers

  • Workload forecasts by territory, season and service type
  • The right split of fixed staff and variable / seasonal crews
  • Schedules shaped to the forecast workload, not the average week
  • What-if scenarios for new territories, contracts and peak seasons

How we start

  2. 2

  3. 3
    Season over season — the plan tunes as the business moves
