Gustora by ANIVA AI, for communities

Early pilot · a few communities at a time

For HOAs, apartment and condo buildings and senior living communities: one private place for your announcements, gatherings and maintenance requests, and for residents helping each other. We're testing it first in one Michigan neighborhood.

What your residents get

📣 Announcements

The pool's schedule, a water shut-off, bingo moving to 3 PM: on everyone's home screen, with the newest marked.

🎉 Gatherings for everyone

Potlucks, game nights and holiday walks that every member sees and can RSVP to, with a head count for the host.

🤝 Neighbors helping neighbors

Rides to the airport or the store (free, or a share of the gas), a hand with something heavy, things to give away, inside your community.

🧁 Home bakers nearby

Breads, pies, cakes and treats from neighbors' home kitchens, under each state's home-kitchen law. Residents see bakers' specialties and typical prices, and can tell them what they'd buy.

🛠️ Maintenance requests

Residents report a dripping faucet or a broken light, and follow it until it's done.

🔒 Privacy, built in

Street addresses and phone numbers stay private, shared only with the neighbors someone is helping.

📘 A guide for residents

How to put Gustora on their phone and use everything your community offers, with a large-print sheet they can print.

What your board or management team gets

🔑 Join code, link and flyer

Residents join with your code. Print a sign-up flyer with a QR code for the lobby, mailroom or activity board.

🚀 Kickoff kit

A plan in four steps and the words to use: a welcome announcement, an email and a text to residents, a newsletter note, two minutes for your meeting, and a large-print sheet for your sign-up table. All written for your community.

✅ You approve who joins

We recommend approval for buildings and senior living, so the code can't be passed on to strangers.

👥 Members and co-admins

See your member list, add co-admins, remove someone, or make a new join code at any time.

🚩 Reports and moderation

Residents can report a post; your admins can hide it from the community with one tap.

📄 Documents and links

Pool rules, meeting minutes and bylaws, linked from wherever you keep them.

🗓️ Printable calendar

Two weeks of community gatherings and pinned notices, in large print for the bulletin board.

🔠 Larger text

Every resident can make Gustora bigger on their phone. Senior living members are offered it when they join.

🏢 Open or closed

Open adds your community to residents' usual neighborhood. Closed keeps posts inside your community.

🧭 One view for managers

Run several buildings? See what's waiting in each: requests to join, reports and open maintenance.

Questions we hear

Is our residents' information private?

Yes. Each resident's street address, phone and email stay private. Only the neighbors they're helping, and the admins for maintenance requests, see what they need. Your community's posts are for its members.

Does Gustora collect HOA dues or rent?

No. Gustora never handles money between neighbors.

Does Gustora do the repairs?

No: maintenance requests go to your own management team, and Gustora carries the message. For emergencies, residents should always call 911 or the building's emergency line.

Will our residents be flooded with notifications?

No. Residents see what's new when they open Gustora. Admins can send an important announcement by text or email from their own phone in one tap.

Can our younger residents join?

Gustora is for adults 18 and older. Children are welcome to enjoy food, rides and gatherings with a parent or guardian.

How do our residents get Gustora on their phones?

There's no app store and nothing for you to install. Each resident opens gustora.web.app, signs up, and adds Gustora to their home screen in about a minute. Your kickoff kit has a ready-to-send email, a large-print how-to sheet, and a checklist for a sign-up table, where you can help neighbors in person. Please let residents know there's nothing to download: an app with a similar name is in the app stores, and it isn't ours.

How do we join the pilot?

Please tell us about your community. We're adding a few communities at a time, so we can get every detail right for each one.

What happens after the pilot?

We'll share pricing well before anything changes. If you'd rather not continue, your community simply pauses: nothing is deleted.
