About the President & Founder

Dr. Mani Manivannan
Logistics & Supply Chain Executive  |  Principal Consultant, ANIVA Enterprises LLC

Dr. Mani Manivannan is a seasoned logistics and supply chain executive with more than 42 years of progressive leadership spanning consulting, defense, automotive, manufacturing, and 3PL/4PL sectors. As President and Principal Consultant of ANIVA Enterprises LLC — a Delaware-based firm he founded in 2007 — he delivers high-impact engagements that drive cost savings, revenue growth, and operational transformation for clients across the transportation, beverage/retail, chemical, oil & gas, and defense industries.

He brings a rare combination of executive depth and analytical precision. He holds a Ph.D. in Industrial Engineering from Penn State and has led teams of 80 to 110 professionals throughout his career. His consulting work is grounded in decades of hands-on executive roles at world-class organizations, including XPO Logistics, BAE Systems, Walker SCM, Rush Group, and Venture Logistics.

Before entering industry he served on the faculty of the School of Industrial and Systems Engineering at Georgia Tech, and later as Adjunct Associate Professor at the Oregon Graduate Institute and Portland State University. He has published some 25 peer-reviewed papers on logistics, supply chain, simulation, artificial intelligence, and robotics, served as a panel member for the National Science Foundation, and was Program Chair of the 1998 Winter Simulation Conference.
